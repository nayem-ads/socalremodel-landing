3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We share your information only with our trusted network of local remodeling specialists to facilitate the delivery of your requested quote.

We implement standard security measures to protect the safety of your personal information when you enter, submit, or access your information.
